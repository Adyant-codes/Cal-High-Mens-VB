Tryouts

2025-2026 Season

Location & Format

All tryouts will be held in the Event Center. All players will try out at the same time with two courts running simultaneously.

Tryout Process

Day 1

Monday, February 9th

  • Arrive at 6:00 PM
  • Check in at the front desk
  • Receive your tryout number
  • Be assigned to a court
  • Begin tryout session
Day 2

Tuesday, February 10th

  • Arrive at 6:00 PM
  • Check in and receive your number
  • Court assignment
  • Continue evaluation process
Day 3

Wednesday, February 11th

  • Arrive at 6:00 PM
  • Check in and receive your number
  • Final evaluation session
  • Results sent via email that evening

You will receive an email notification indicating whether you made a team and which team you've been placed on.

  • Athletic shoes
  • Water bottle

Attendance Required

All three days of tryouts are mandatory. Players who miss any session will not be considered for team selection unless prior arrangements have been made with the coaching staff.
